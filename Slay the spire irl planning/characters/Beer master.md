
**concept**
Many cards force you to take a small sip of beer/cider. Generally not as much at once but consistent and repeated.

Starting deck
5x [[strike]]
5x [[defend]]
1x [[Alcoholic rage]] (apply 2 vulnerable, take a sip)

Findable cards
[[Shared fate]] (Pick another drinker, drink whenever they drink this combat and gain 1 strength)
[[Slurred words]] (Deal 8 damage, if you've drank this turn attacks again)
Snakebite (Retain, Deal 30 damage, 3 cost)
