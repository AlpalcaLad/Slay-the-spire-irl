
**concept**
Many cards force you to take a small sip of beer/cider. Generally not as much at once but consistent and repeated.

Starting deck
4x [[strike]]
4x [[defend]]
1x [[Alcoholic rage]] (apply 2 vulnerable, take a sip)

Findable cards
[[Corona and Lime]] (Deal 1 damage, draw 1 card, 1 cost ATTACK, COMMON)
[[Brewdog]] (Deal 1 damage, if your last card drank, gain 1 energy, 1 cost ATTACK)
[[Inch's]] (Deal 1 damage to all enemies, 1 cost ATTACK, COMMON)
[[Relaxing pint]] (Heal 2, take a sip, exhaust 1 cost SKILL)
[[On tap]] (Draw 1 card, if it's named after a drink draw 2 more, 1 cost SKILL)
[[Catch up]] (Gain 1 energy, take a sip, 0 cost SKILL, COMMON)
[[Chug]] (Deal 4 damage, take 4 sips, exhaust 1 cost ATTACK)
[[Slurred words]] (Deal 1 damage, if you've drank this turn attacks again, 1 cost ATTACK)
[[Snakebite]] (Retain, Deal 5 damage, 3 cost ATTACK, RARE)
[[Wingman]] (Deal 1 damage, increase this by 1 and put on top of the draw pile of the player to the right, 1 cost, ATTACK, RARE)
[[Beer jacket]] (gain 2 block, take a sip, 1 cost, SKILL)
