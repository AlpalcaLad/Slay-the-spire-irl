
**concept**
Many cards force you to take a small sip of beer/cider. Generally not as much at once but consistent and repeated.

Starting deck
5x [[strike]]
5x [[defend]]
1x [[Alcoholic rage]] (apply 2 vulnerable, take a sip)

Findable cards
[[Shared fate]] (gain 2 strength, Pick another drinker, drink whenever they drink this combat, 2 cost POWER, RARE)
[[Slurred words]] (Deal 1 damage, if you've drank this turn attacks again, 1 cost ATTACK)
[[Snakebite]] (Retain, Deal 5 damage, 3 cost ATTACK, RARE)
[[Wingman]] (Deal 1 damage, increase this by 1 and put in the draw pile of the player to the right, 1 cost, ATTACK)
[[Beer jacket]] (gain 2 block, take a sip, 1 cost, SKILL)
[[Ring of fire]] (everyone gains energy and takes a sip, exhaust, 0 cost SKILL)
[[Split the G]] (Gain 1 strength for every vulnerable the enemy has, halve your drink. 2 cost POWER, RARE)
[[Hells]] (Draw 2 cards, 0 cost, take a sip SKILL)
[[Break the seal]] (Discard any number of cards and draw that many, 1 cost, SKILL)
[[Tacky chunder]] (Exhaust a card in your hand, gain 2 block, 1 cost, SKILL)
[[Beer belly]] (If you've drank this turn, gain 3 block, 2 cost, SKILL)
