
**concept**
Use brew cards to add things to a drink
Starting card makes you drink what you’ve brewed, which has an effect based on what you’ve brewed

**starting cards**
4x [[strike]]
4x [[defend]]
1x [[Clear(ly) strong]] (brews splash of vodka, 0 cost SKILL)
1x [[lemon up]] (brews shot of lemonade, 0 cost SKILL)
1x [[Down the hatch]] (drink everything brewed, 2 cost SKILL)

**findable cards**
[[Bump the flavour]] (brew splash of [[squash]], costs 1, SKILL)
[[See sunrise]] (brew shot of [[orange juice]], costs 1, SKILL)
[[Red moon]] (brew shot of [[cranberry juice]], costs 0, SKILL)
[[Schnapp to it]] (brew splash of [[peach schnapps]])
[[Rumaway]] (brew splash of [[rum]], exhaust costs 2, SKILL, RARE)
[[Spin the bottle]] (brew two random splashes, 1 cost SKILL)
[[Roulette]] (Discard your hand, draw that many cards, Exhaust, 0 cost, SKILL) 
[[Heavyweight]] (Whenever you brew, gain 1 block, 1 cost POWER)
[[The bill]] (Deal 3 damage, costs 1 more for each thing in your cup. Costs 0, ATTACK)
[[What next]] (Whenever you down the hatch, draw 2 cards, POWER)
[[Taste test]] (Gain 2 energy, take a sip, costs 0, SKILL)
[[Key ingredients]] (Choose any two skills from your draw pile and add them to your hand, 1 cost, SKILL, RARE)
