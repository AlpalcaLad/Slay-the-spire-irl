
**concept**
Use brew cards to add things to a drink
Starting card makes you drink what you’ve brewed, which has an effect based on what you’ve brewed

**starting cards**
4x [[strike]]
4x [[defend]]
1x [[Clear(ly) strong]] (brews splash of vodka)
1x [[lemon up]] (brews shot of lemonade)
1x [[Down the hatch]] (drink everything brewed)

**findable cards**
[[Bump the flavour]] (brew splash of [[squash]])
[[See sunrise]] (brew shot of [[orange juice]])
[[Red moon]] (brew shot of [[cranberry juice]])
[[Schnapp to it]] (brew splash of [[peach schnapps]])
[[Rumaway]] (brew splash of [[rum]])
