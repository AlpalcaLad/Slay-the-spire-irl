Concept 
Lots of buffs and multiplayer cards and ability to make potions

**starting cards**
3x [[strike]]
3x [[defend]]
1x [[Supply bag]] (Give a player with space a random weak potion, 1 cost SKILL)
1x [[Not for me]] (You don't need to drink the next time, 1 cost SKILL)

Findable cards
[[Carry]] (Give another player 2 block, 1 cost SKILL)
[[Hit and run]] (Deal 2 damage, gain 2 block, 2 cost ATTACK)
[[The good stuff]] (Give all players with space a random potion, exhaust, 1 cost SKILL, RARE)
[[Raid trunk]] (Draw 3 cards, exhaust, 1 cost SKILL)
[[Be responsible]] (Exhaust a card in another players hand, 1 cost SKILL)
[[Offer lift]] (Another player can draw a card of their choice, 1 cost SKILL)
[[Cherry pick]] (Discard any number of cards from your draw pile, 2 cost SKILL)
[[Sober focus]] (Gain 3 energy if you haven't drunk this combat, 0 cost SKILL, RARE)
[[Empty tank]] (Gain 2 block X times, X cost SKILL)
[[Line the stomach]] (All players gain 1 block, 1 cost SKILL)
[[Check in]] (Another player heals X, X cost SKILL)
[[Maybe just one]] (Gain 2 energy, take a sip, 0 cost SKILL, COMMON)
[[Rules broken]] (Draw 1 for each drink you've had this combat, 1 cost SKILL)
[[Believe in you]] (Another player gains 2 energy, 0 cost SKILL, COMMON)
[[Freshen up]] (Exhaust any number of cards in your hand, 1 cost SKILL)
