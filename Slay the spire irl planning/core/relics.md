common relics
Anchor - start each combat with 2 block
Horn cleat - on your second turn gain 3 block
Captains wheel - on your third turn gain 4 block
Bag of prep - start each combat with 2 extra cards
Blood vial - Heal 1 at the start of each combat
Happy flower - every 3 turns, gain 1 energy
Lantern - Gain 1 energy on the first turn of each combat
Orichalcum - If you end your turn without block, gain 1 block
Potion belt - you can have up to 2 extra potions at a time
Strawberry -  on pickup, gain 2 extra max hp
Mercury Hourglass - at the start of your turn deal 1 damage to all enemies
Pear - on pickup, gain 1 extra max hp and heal 2
Strike dummy - strike cards do 1 extra damage

rare relics
Lizard tail - when you would die, heal to 50% (works once)
Old coin - on pickup get 3 money
Peace pipe - When you rest you may remove 1 card from your deck permanently
Unceasing top - when you have no cards in your hand, draw 1
Lees waffle - on pickup, Gain 1 extra max hp and fully heal
Sling of Courage - start each non boss combat with 1 strength
Empty cage - Upon pickup remove 2 cards from your deck permanently 
Turnip - You can no longer become frail
